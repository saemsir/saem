Preparation built around four goals.

Physics is one subject, but NEET, JEE, MIT admissions, and Olympiads each test it differently. Here's how the focus shifts for each.

NEET

NEET physics rewards speed and accuracy across mechanics, electricity, and modern physics. Classes are built to make that pace feel normal.

JEE

JEE physics rewards students who can apply a concept somewhere it hasn't been taught before. Preparation is built around unfamiliar problems.

MIT & global admissions

Global applications look for genuine intellectual engagement with physics alongside standardised coursework.

Olympiads

Olympiad physics focuses on multi-step reasoning, unfamiliar setups, and problems with more than one path to a solution.
