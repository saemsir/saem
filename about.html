About Saem Physics

Physics is my Business — a personal teaching practice built around one goal: genuine understanding, for students preparing for NEET, JEE, MIT admissions, and Olympiads.

Refined academic content, built with intent.

Saem Physics is dedicated to offering the most refined academic content — lessons built around genuine understanding, not memorised shortcuts. Every topic is approached the way it will actually show up on the exam: as a problem to reason through, rather than a formula to recall.

How it works

Saem Physics is built around direct access — not a support ticket, not a rotating roster of teaching assistants. Questions go straight to Telegram or email and get a real answer. Notes are curated and shared personally, not auto-generated. Every class is taught with the exam in mind, but the goal underneath it is always the same: understanding physics well enough that the exam stops feeling like the hard part.

What the content covers

Classes and notes are built around the NCERT syllabus for Class 11 and 12 — the foundation both NEET and JEE draw from — with chapter-wise revision, previous-year-question walkthroughs, and a focus on the topics that carry the most weight under exam conditions. Physics content is also available in Hindi for Bihar Board students.
