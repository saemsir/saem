Preparation built around four goals.

Physics is one subject, but NEET, JEE, MIT admissions, and Olympiads each test it differently. Here's how the focus shifts for each.

NEET

NEET physics rewards speed and accuracy across mechanics, electricity, and modern physics — the sections it weights most heavily. Classes here are built to make that pace feel normal, with enough practice that recall turns into instinct.

JEE

JEE physics — especially at the Advanced stage — rewards students who can apply a concept somewhere it hasn't been taught before. Preparation is built around solving unfamiliar problems, not just recognising familiar ones.

MIT & global admissions

A global application looks for genuine intellectual engagement with physics, not just correct answers — alongside the standardised coursework many applications still expect. Support here covers both.

Olympiads

Olympiad physics is its own discipline: multi-step reasoning, unfamiliar setups, and problems built to have more than one path to a solution. Preparation focuses on the habits that transfer across Olympiad-style exams generally.
